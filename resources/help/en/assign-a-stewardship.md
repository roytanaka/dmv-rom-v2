# Assign a Stewardship

Choose which Groups hold an org-wide job. You need the super-tier grant.

A Stewardship gives its authority to every Member of the Groups that hold it. Two Stewardships show on the page:

- **Member administration** lets Members edit every Member's record and open the all-DMV reports. Exactly one Group holds it.
- **Org-wide sender** lets Members email every Member, every Chair and the Board. Any number of Groups can hold it, or none.

## Move a Stewardship

1. In the Left Side Bar, select **DMV Settings** under Officer Tools.
2. Select **Stewardships**.
3. Find the card for the Stewardship you want to change.
4. For member administration, choose the new Group from the list.
5. For org-wide sender, tick each Group that should hold it. Untick each Group that should not.
6. Read the panel under your choice. **Gain the authority** lists the Members who get it. **Lose the authority** lists the Members who lose it.
7. Select **Save** on that card. Save stays off until you change something. **Undo change** puts the saved choice back.

The change takes effect on each Member's next page.

> **Note:** Member administration always has a holder. Move it to another Group; you cannot leave it empty.

Each card shows who last changed it, and when. A Chair of an active Group can always email org-wide, so a Chair never shows under Gain or Lose for org-wide sender.

## What next

A Group that holds a Stewardship cannot be archived. Move its Stewardships first.
