# Read your hours for the fiscal year

Read your hours across every Group for one fiscal year.

## Open your hours

1. Select **My Hours** in the top bar.
2. The page opens on the current fiscal year.

![My Hours on the current fiscal year: a Group card with Scheduled, Extra, and Total columns, one row per month](01.png)

## Read one Group

Each Group you have hours in shows as a card. Each row is a month. The columns split your hours three ways. Scheduled counts the Shifts you signed up for. Extra counts the hours you recorded by hand. Total adds the two together.

The **Year to date** row at the foot of each card totals the year.

## Read a past year

Select a year in the **Fiscal year** picker at the top. The page reloads on that year. The fiscal year runs 1 April to 31 March.

## What next

To add hours to a Group, read "Record extra hours for a month".
