# Running the renewal

Each year, the Records office opens the Renewal, sets the reminders and records who has paid.

## Set up the renewal

1. Select **Officer Tools**, then **Members**.
2. Select **Renewal settings**.
3. Tick **Show the renewal** to open the Renewal. Untick it to close it.
4. Type the year the Renewal is for in **Renewal year**.
5. Under **Active reminders**, pick the **Send date**.
6. Type the **First reminder** and the **Second reminder** texts.
7. Under **Sustaining reminders**, pick the **Send date** and type the two texts.
8. To send a form with the Sustaining reminders, choose a file under **Attachment**.
9. Under **External addresses**, type any mailbox outside the app that gets a copy of renewal Notices. Put one address on each line.
10. Select **Save**.

The page shows who last saved it, and when.

> **Note:** The first reminder goes six weeks after the send date and the second goes ten weeks after it.

## Watch the season

The top of the page counts two groups for the renewal year:

- **Renewed for**: Members who sent in the renewal form for that year.
- **Paid through**: Members whose paid-through year is the renewal year.

A Member who renewed but is not yet paid through the renewal year has not paid.

## Record a payment

1. Open the Member's record from a Member list.
2. Under **Dues**, type the year in **Paid-through year**. Or select **+1** to add one year.
3. Select **Save**.
