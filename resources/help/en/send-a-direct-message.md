# Send a Direct message to a Member

Write to one other Member without knowing their email address.

## Send the message

1. Open the Directory from the top bar.
2. Select the Member you want to reach.
3. Select the **Message** button in the profile header.
4. Write your subject and message.
5. Select **Send**.

The app delivers your message. Your own address rides along as the reply address, so the Member can write back to you.

> **Note:** You cannot message yourself. The **Message** button is hidden on your own profile.
