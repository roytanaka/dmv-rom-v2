# Update your profile and photo

Keep your name, contact details, and photo up to date.

## Open your profile

1. Select your name in the top bar.
2. Select **My Profile**.

## Change your details

1. Edit any field: name, email, phone numbers, or home address.
2. Select **Save**.

> **Note:** To change your email, you also type your current password. This field appears only when you change the email.

## Add or change your photo

1. Select the file button under **Profile photo**.
2. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image, up to 5 MB.
3. Select **Save**.

To take your photo down, select **Remove photo**.

## What next

Your name and photo show in the Directory and on your profile. To change your password, read "Change your password".
