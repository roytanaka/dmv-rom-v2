# Add a group tour

Add a tour a client has booked, so your Group sees it on the schedule. You need the Booker or Chair role.

A group tour is a tour a client books for a date, such as a school or a tour company. The app puts each group tour on its month's group-tour schedule.

## Add a group tour

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.
3. Select **Add group tour**.
4. Type the client's name. The field suggests clients your Group has booked before.
5. Pick the tour and the booking type.
6. Enter the date, the start and end times, and the number of docents needed.
7. Enter the expected visitors.
8. Fill in the group leader, order number, order date and comments if you have them.
9. Select **Add**.

The first group tour in a month creates that month's group-tour schedule. The schedule is published straight away. Later group tours that month join it.

> **Note:** Set the group-tour shift kind on the **Settings** tab first. The form refuses a group tour until it is set.

## What each reader sees

Open the month's group-tour schedule from the **Scheduling** tab. Each group tour shows its time, tour and seats.

- Members of your Group also see the client, visitors, booking type, leader and comments.
- Bookers, the Statistician and the Chair also see the order number, the order date and Earned.
- Anyone else sees only the time, tour and seats.
