# Change your password

Change the password you sign in with.

## Change it

1. Select your initials or photo in the top bar.
2. Select **My Profile**.
3. Select **Password** in the sidebar.

    ![The Password page: Current password, New password, Confirm password, and the Save password button](01.png)

4. Type your current password.
5. Type your new password, then type it again to confirm.
6. Select **Save password**.

The page confirms with "Saved" when the change goes through.

> **Tip:** Use a long password you do not use anywhere else.

## What next

Your new password takes effect at once. To update your other details, read [Update your profile and photo](update-your-profile).
