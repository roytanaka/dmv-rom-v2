# Post a news item

Post news for a Group to the whole-DMV feed. You need the News Editor role.

## Write a post

1. Select **News** in the top bar.
2. Select **New post**.
3. Choose the Group you post for in **Posting group**.
4. Type a **Title** and a **Body**.
5. Select **Publish**.

Your post appears at the top of the feed for every Member.

## Edit or remove a post

Your own posts carry **Edit** and **Delete** controls. Select **Edit** to change the title or body. Select **Delete** to remove the post. Deleting asks you to confirm first.

## What next

Your post shows in the feed like any other. Read "Read news" to see the reader's view.
