# Change or delete a group tour

Change a group tour when the client's plans change, or delete it when they cancel. You need the Booker, Statistician or Chair role.

## Change a group tour

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.
3. Open the month's group-tour schedule.
4. Find the group tour and select **Edit**.
5. Change what you need: the client, tour, type, date, times, docents needed or any other field.
6. Select **Save**.

If you pick a date in another month, the group tour moves to that month's group-tour schedule. Its sign-ups move with it. If that month has no schedule yet, the app creates and publishes it.

If you change the tour, the sign-ups switch to the new tour.

> **Note:** Docents needed can't drop below the number of people already signed up. Remove a sign-up first.

## Delete a group tour

1. Find the group tour on its schedule and select **Delete**.
2. Read the confirmation. It names everyone signed up for the group tour.
3. Select **Delete** in the confirmation. The group tour and its sign-ups are removed.

The month's group-tour schedule stays, even when it's empty. A Scheduler can delete it only once it holds no group tours.
