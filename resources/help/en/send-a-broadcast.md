# Send a Broadcast to your Group

Email everyone in your Group at once.

## Send the Broadcast

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select **Email** in the tab strip.

    ![The Email menu open under the tab strip: each Audience with the number of Members it reaches](01.png)

3. Select **Whole group** from the menu.

    ![The Who step for Whole group: one name for each Member, each with an ×](02.png)

4. Check the **To** list. Remove anyone with the × beside their name.
5. Select **Next**.
6. Write your subject and message.

    ![The Message step: the Audience, the subject and message filled in, and Send](03.png)

7. Select **Send**.

    ![After you send: Queued for 23 members, and a note that you get a copy](04.png)

The app sends one email to each Member. You get a copy once delivery is done.

> **Note:** The Broadcast goes out under your Group's name. Replies come to you.
