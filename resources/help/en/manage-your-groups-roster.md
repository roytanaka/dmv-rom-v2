# Manage your Group's roster

Add Members to your Group, set the roles they hold, and remove those who leave. You need the Secretary or Chair role.

## Add a Member

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Members** tab.

    ![The Members tab: Show past members and Add member above the roster, and a menu at the end of each row](01.png)

3. Select **Add member**.

    ![The Add member dialog: two Members checked show above the search box, and the Roles are not available](02.png)

4. Type a name in **Search all members**.
5. Check the box next to each Member to add. Each Member you check shows above the search box.
6. Set the **Standing** in the Group. It applies to every Member you checked.
7. Check any **Roles** the Member holds.
8. Select **Add to group**. The button shows how many Members you add.

> **Note:** You can set roles for one Member at a time. When you check two or more Members, the **Roles** are not available.

To set roles for those Members, add them, then select **Manage** on each row.

To remove a Member from your selection, select the **×** next to their name above the search box.

## Change a Member's roles or standing

1. Open the three-dot menu at the end of the Member's row.

    ![A row's menu: Manage, Resign member, Tours, and Remove (added in error)](03.png)

2. Select **Manage**.

    ![The Manage membership dialog: Standing, the leave dates, and Roles](04.png)

3. Change the **Standing**, the leave dates, or the **Roles**.
4. Select **Save**.

## See a Member's DMV standing

The **Standing** column shows the Member's standing in your Group. It also shows the Member's standing in the whole DMV, for example **On leave**. The DMV standing shows only when it is not Active. It has the same badge as on the Member's profile.

The two standings are separate. A change to the DMV standing does not change the standing in your Group. To change the standing in your Group, select **Manage** on the row.

## Remove a Member

Open the three-dot menu at the end of the Member's row.

- Select **Resign member** for someone who is leaving. Their history is kept, and you can reinstate them later.
- Select **Remove (added in error)** only for someone you added by mistake. This deletes the row for good.

> **Note:** Check **Show past members** to see Members you have resigned.

## What next

Roles you set here decide what each Member can do in the Group. To record a meeting, read [Record a meeting](record-a-meeting).
