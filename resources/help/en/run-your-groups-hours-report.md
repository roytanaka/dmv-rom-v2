# Run your Group's hours report

Read every Member's hours for your Group across a fiscal year. You need the Statistician or Chair role.

## Open the report

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Hours** tab.

    ![The Hours tab, as a Statistician: View the group hours report above the entry form](01.png)

3. Select **View the group hours report**.

    ![The hours report: one row per Member, one column per month, and the year-to-date total](02.png)

The report opens on the current fiscal year.

## Read the report

Each row is one Member. Each column is one month, from April to March. The last column is the Member's year-to-date total.

Two rows sit below the table:

- **This group** totals the Group on its own.
- **Including sub-groups** adds every Group beneath it.

## Change the fiscal year

Select a year under **Fiscal year** to read an earlier one. The table reloads for that year.

## See other views

The links across the top open other cuts of the same hours: **By month**, **Member history**, **Extra hours**, and **Meeting hours**.

![The By month view: one month's entries across the Group, Member by Member](03.png)

> **Note:** A Chair or Statistician of a parent Group reads this report too. The role reaches down the whole tree.

## What next

Select **Print** for a paper copy, or read [Export a report as CSV](export-a-report-as-csv) to open the numbers in a spreadsheet.
