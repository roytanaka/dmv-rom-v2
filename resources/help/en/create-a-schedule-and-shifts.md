# Create a Schedule and its Shifts

Open a Schedule for your Group, then add the Shifts Members sign up to staff. You need the Scheduler or Chair role.

## Create the Schedule

1. Open your Group from the rail.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.

    ![The Scheduling tab, as a Scheduler: the New schedule button above the list of schedules](01.png)

3. Select **New schedule**.

    ![The New schedule dialog: Name, Start date, End date, and Description](02.png)

4. Type a **Name**, a **Start date**, and an **End date**.
5. Add a **Description** if it helps. This field is optional.
6. Select **Save**.

The Schedule starts as a draft. Only your Group's Schedulers and Chairs can see a draft.

## Add Shifts

1. Open the Schedule you just made.

    ![A draft Schedule: the Draft badge and Publish at the top, and New shift below](03.png)

2. Select **New shift**.

    ![The New shift dialog: Start, End, Capacity, Kind, and Audience](04.png)

3. Set the **Start** and **End** times.
4. Set the **Capacity**, the number of Members the Shift holds.
5. Choose a **Kind** if your Group uses them. Leave it on **No kind** otherwise.
6. Choose the **Audience**: **Group members** or **Open to all**.
7. Select **Save**.

Repeat for each Shift. To add many at once, read _Add or remove many Shifts at once_.

## Publish the Schedule

Select **Publish** at the top of the Schedule when the Shifts are ready. Members can now see the Schedule and sign up.

> **Note:** You can un-publish or delete a Schedule only while no one has signed up.

## What next

Members sign up on their own. To place a named Member yourself, read _Assign a Member to a Shift_.
