# What Support is for

Support holds the tools a Support operator uses to test the app. They run on staging and local only.

## The Role-switcher

The Role-switcher is a button in the bottom-right corner of every page. Select it to become a Persona. You then see the app with that Persona's roles.

## Personas

A Persona is a made-up Member who exists only for testing. The Personas cover every role and standing in the app.

## Return to yourself

While you act as a Persona, a toolbar shows who you are. Select **Stop** to return to your own account.

> **Note:** You need the Support-operator marker to see these tools. They never appear in production.

Start with [Use the Role-switcher](use-the-role-switcher). Then read [Return to yourself after impersonating](return-to-yourself).
