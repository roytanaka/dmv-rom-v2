# What Invitations are for

An Invitation asks Members to an occasion, such as the Annual Meeting or a small outing. Select **Invitations** in the top bar to find yours.

## My invitations

Every Member has this tab. It lists the Open Invitations you are on, with your Reply beside each one. Read [Reply to an Invitation](reply-to-an-invitation) for the steps.

## Hosting

Members with Full access also see the **Hosting** tab. The Member who creates an Invitation is its Host. Only the Host and the super-tier see the guest list and the Replies.

Hosting has these parts:

- [Create an Invitation](create-an-invitation)
- [Pick your Guests](pick-your-guests)
- [Send an Invitation](send-an-invitation)
- [Track the Replies](track-the-replies)
- [Set a Reply for a Guest](set-a-reply-for-a-guest)
- [Email Accepted Guests](email-accepted-guests)

## Draft, Open, and Closed

A new Invitation is a Draft. Only its Host sees it. Sending moves it to Open, and Guests can then reply. It is Closed once its date has passed.

## Reminders

Each Guest who accepts gets a Reminder by email 4 days before the occasion. A Guest who accepts later than that still gets one.
