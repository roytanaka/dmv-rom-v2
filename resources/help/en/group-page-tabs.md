# The tabs on a Group page

Every Group page holds the same set of tabs across the top. Here is what each one is for.

## Overview

The first tab. It shows the Group's description, its officers, and a few facts about the Group.

![A Group page on Overview: the description and the officers, with the tabs across the top](01.png)

## Members

The Group's roster. It lists everyone in the Group and the role each person holds.

![The Members tab: the roster with names, roles, and standing](02.png)

## Meetings

The Group's meetings, with their agendas and minutes. This tab shows only when the Group holds meetings.

![The Meetings tab on a Group that holds meetings, with its own set of tabs](03.png)

## Scheduling

The Group's schedules and shifts. Sign up for a shift here. This tab shows only when the Group runs scheduling.

![The Scheduling tab: your sign-ups at the top, then the Group's schedules](04.png)

## Hours

Your hours in this Group, month by month. You can also record extra hours here.

![The Hours tab: the Record extra hours form, one row per month](05.png)

> **Note:** A Group turns on only the tabs it needs, so two Groups may not show the same set. A tab marked **Soon** is not ready yet.

## What next

Open the **Scheduling** tab, then read _Sign up for a shift_.
