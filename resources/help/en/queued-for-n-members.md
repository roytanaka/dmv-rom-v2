# What "queued for N Members" means

Understand the message you see after you send an email.

## After you send

The app does not send your email the moment you select **Send**. It lines up one email for each Member and sends them in batches. The composer then shows **Queued for N members.**

![After a send to the Group's officers: Queued for 5 members, and the delivery note](01.png)

## What happens next

- A small send lands within a minute.
- A Broadcast to a large Audience can take up to an hour.
- You get a copy once every email is out.
- The copy names anyone the app could not reach.

> **Note:** The app skips a Member with email switched off. Your copy names everyone it could not reach.

## What next

If a message never seems to arrive, ask a super-tier officer to check the Mail status page.
