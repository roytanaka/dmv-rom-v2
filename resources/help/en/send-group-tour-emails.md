# Send group tour emails

Ask docents to sign up for a group tour, or tell the docents on it that it is confirmed. You need the Booker or Chair role.

There are two group tour emails:

- A **request** goes to every Member who may give the tour. For a tour open to all, that is the whole roster. It asks them to sign up.
- A **confirmation** goes to the Members signed up. It lists everyone on the group tour.

Both show the tour, date, time, visitors, client, leader and comments. Replies go to you.

## When the app sends them

Adding a group tour sends a request. If the group tour is already full, it sends a confirmation instead.

## Send one again

1. Open the month's group-tour schedule from the **Scheduling** tab.
2. Find the group tour.
3. Select **Send request** or **Send confirmation**.

A short line confirms the email is queued. It goes out within a few minutes.

> **Note:** Members who turned off email do not get these emails.

## Copy every email to one address

Your Group can copy every group tour email to one address, such as a staff contact. Set it under **Copy every group-tour email to** on the **Group tours** card of the **Settings** tab.
