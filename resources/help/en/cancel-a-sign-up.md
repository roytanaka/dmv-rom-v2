# Cancel a sign-up

Give back a seat on a shift you can no longer work.

## Drop your seat

1. Open the Group and its **Scheduling** tab.
2. Select the schedule that holds the shift.
3. Find the shift with your name on it.
4. Select **Drop**.

Your name leaves the shift, and the seat opens for someone else.

> **Note:** You can drop a seat any time before the shift starts. Your Group's Scheduler gets an email when you do.

## What next

Sign up for another shift, or read _The shifts you still owe a number for_.
