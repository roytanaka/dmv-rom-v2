# Write your own shift

Write a shift for yourself when your Group lets its Members schedule their own. The same step signs you up.

## Write the shift

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.
3. Open a published Schedule.
4. Select **Write my shift**.

    > **Note:** The button shows only when your Group is self-serve. Ask a Scheduler if you do not see it.

5. Choose a **Station**.
6. Set the **Start time**. Pick any quarter hour on the day.
7. Choose the number of **Units**. The form shows when your shift ends.
8. Add one or more **Objects** if your Group hands them out.
9. Select **Save**.

Your shift joins the Schedule with your name on it.

## Clear the station warning

Another member may already hold that station at that time. The form warns you and asks to continue.

1. Read the warning.
2. Select **Continue** to keep your start, or **Cancel** to change it.

Two Members may share a station on purpose, so this is a warning, not a block.

## Edit or delete before it starts

You own the shift until it starts.

1. Find your shift on the Schedule.
2. Select **Edit** to change the station, start, units, or Objects.
3. Select **Delete** to remove the shift. This also removes you from it.

After the start, ask a Scheduler to make any change.

## Sign out after the shift

Your shift ends at the start plus its units. The sign-out panel opens near that time.

1. Type the number of visitors you served.
2. Select **Sign out**.

## What next

Read _Cancel a sign-up_ for shifts a Scheduler wrote. Read _Record your visitor count after a shift_ for the sign-out details.
