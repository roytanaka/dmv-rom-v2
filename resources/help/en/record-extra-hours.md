# Record extra hours for a month

Record the hours you spent helping a Group outside your scheduled Shifts.

## Record your hours

1. Open a Group from the Dashboard or the rail.
2. Select the **Hours** tab.

    ![The Hours tab of a Group: the Record extra hours form, with Hours to add and Add hours for this month and last month](01.png)

3. Find the month you want. The form offers this month and last month.
4. Type the hours in **Hours to add**.
5. Select **Add hours**.

The hours are added to what is already on file for that month. **Visitors to add** counts people you served outside a Shift, not hours. Leave it empty if it does not apply.

> **Note:** Scheduled Shifts and meetings are already counted. Add only the hours you worked outside them.

## Fix a mistake

Type a negative number in **Hours to add** to lower the total. The Group never drops below zero.

## What next

Your recorded hours show in "Your hours in this group" below the form. Open My Hours to read your full year across every Group.
