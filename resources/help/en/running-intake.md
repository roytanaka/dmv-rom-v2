# Running intake

The Membership Group moves each Applicant from application to Provisional Member. Change the status on the Applicant record as the case moves on.

## Open the Applicants list

1. Select **Officer Tools**, then **Applicants**. Member administration selects **Members**, then **Applicants**.
2. The list shows **All**: every case except Incomplete and Ready to go active.

    ![The Applicants list with the status, dates, email and mentor of each case](01.png)

3. Choose a status filter to see one stage.

    ![The Applicants list filtered to the Accepted cases](02.png)

4. Type a last name in the search box to find one case.
5. Select a name to open the Applicant record.

> **Note:** The Membership Chair edits every Applicant. Other Membership members edit the Applicants they mentor and read the rest. Any Chair can read them.

## Move a case on

1. On the Applicant record, choose the new **Status**.

    ![An Applicant record with the Status, Placement and Mentor selects](03.png)

2. For **Incomplete**, choose a **Reason** as well.
3. Choose a **Placement** and a **Mentor** if they have changed.
4. Add a line in **New note** to record what happened.
5. Select **Save**. The status date moves to today.

## Accept an Applicant

1. Set the **Status** to **Accepted** or **Placed**.
2. Select **Save**. The app creates a Provisional Member from the Applicant.

The new Member gets the names, contact details, ROM membership, emergency contact, mentor, experience, languages and notes. Their entry year is this year. The Applicant stays on the list and links to the Member.

If a Member already has the same email and name, the app updates that Member instead. Their Category stays as it was.

> **Note:** If a Member under another name has the email, the save stops. Correct the name or the email, then save again.

## Who hears about it

The app sends an email Notice when the status moves. Nothing goes to the applicant.

- **Accepted**: everyone in the Records office and the mentor.
- **Placed**: the mentor.
- **Yellow card completed**: the Treasurer of the DMV Executive.
- **Incomplete**, once accepted: the mentor, with the reason.

A Member with the no-email flag gets no Notice.

## What next

The Records office opens the new Member from [Member lists and the Member record](member-lists-and-the-member-record).
