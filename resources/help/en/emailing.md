# Emailing

The app sends every message for you. You never see another Member's address, and no one sees yours.

## Broadcast

An officer writes a Broadcast and sends it to an Audience. Rich text, up to two attachments, in one language. The Broadcast goes out under the Group's name, and replies come to the officer who wrote it.

## Direct message

Any Member writes a Direct message to one other Member, from the Directory or a profile. It shares the same composer as a Broadcast. Its Audience is the one Member.

## Audience

An Audience is who a Broadcast goes to, such as your whole Group or the Group's officers. The app builds the list of Members when you send. You name the Audience; you never type addresses.

## Reminder

A Reminder is an automatic email about your own upcoming Sign-up. Your Group turns Reminders on and sets how many days ahead they go.

## Notice

A Notice is an automatic email that an event sends, such as a cancelled Sign-up. It goes to the officers who need to know.

## The no-email flag

Records can switch off all mail to a Member. A Member with the flag gets no Broadcast, Direct message, Reminder, or Notice.

## When mail arrives

Mail leaves in batches, not the moment you send. A small message lands within a minute. A Broadcast to your whole Group can take up to an hour.
