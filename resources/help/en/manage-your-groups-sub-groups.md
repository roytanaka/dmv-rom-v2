# Manage your Group's sub-groups

Create, rename, move and archive the sub-groups below your Group, and run their rosters. You need the Secretary or Chair role.

A sub-group is any Group below yours, at any depth. You cannot rename, move or archive your own Group. The President's office does that.

## Open your sub-groups

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Settings** tab.
3. Under **Sub-groups**, select **Manage sub-groups**.

    ![The Settings tab with the Sub-groups card and its Manage sub-groups link on top](01.png)

**DMV Settings** opens on your Group. You can also open it from **DMV Settings** under Officer Tools in the Left Side Bar.

The page lists your Group and every sub-group below it. Your Group's row reads **Your Group**. Archived sub-groups stay in the list with an **Archived** badge.

![DMV Settings for the Docents Chair: Docents marked Your Group, with Sections and three archived sub-groups below it](02.png)

## Create a sub-group

1. Select **Create a sub-group**.

    ![The Create a sub-group dialog: a name, Docents as the parent, and the Chair search over the Docents members](03.png)

2. Enter a name. Two sub-groups under the same parent cannot share a name.
3. Choose the parent. It can be your Group or any active sub-group below it.
4. To name the Chair now, search the parent Group's members and pick one. You can leave the Chair empty.
5. Select **Create**.

The new sub-group's page opens in DMV Settings. The Chair you picked joins it as a Full member with the Chair role. The sub-group starts with Meetings and Documents on.

> **Note:** Ask the President's office to switch on anything else, such as Scheduling.

## Rename or move a sub-group

1. Select the sub-group's name in the list.

    ![The Sections page in DMV Settings: Name and Parent, the Archive card, and Save changes](04.png)

2. Change the **Name**, or choose a new **Parent**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

You can move a sub-group only within your own Group. It cannot go under itself or under one of its own sub-groups.

## Archive a sub-group

Archive a sub-group when it stops meeting. It leaves the Left Side Bar. Members can still read everything in it, but nobody can change it.

1. Open the sub-group from the list.
2. Under **Archive**, select **Archive this Group**.
3. Select **Archive** to confirm.

    ![The Archive Sections? confirmation with Cancel and Archive](05.png)

To bring it back, open it from the list and select **Unarchive** in the banner.

Sub-groups are never deleted. Archive one instead.

## Manage a sub-group's members

1. Open the sub-group from the list.
2. Select **Open the Group page**.
3. Select the **Members** tab.

    ![The Sections Members tab as the Docents Chair: Add member above the roster, and Rosa Delgado as Chair](06.png)

You manage this roster the same way as your own Group's. You can add members, change their standing, assign roles such as Chair, and resign them.

> **Note:** Running a sub-group's roster does not open its Meetings, Documents or About text to you. To read or write them, add yourself to the sub-group. You then show on its roster.
