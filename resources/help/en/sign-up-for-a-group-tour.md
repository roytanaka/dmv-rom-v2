# Sign up for a group tour

Take a seat on a tour a client has booked, and find a substitute if you can no longer give it.

## Sign up

1. Open your Group and its **Scheduling** tab.
2. Open the month's group-tour schedule.
3. Find a group tour with an open seat.
4. Select **Sign up**.

Your name joins the group tour. The app records the tour the client booked.

> **Note:** You need an active qualification for the tour, unless the tour is open to all. Without one, the **Sign up** button does not show.

## Find a substitute

You cannot drop a seat on a group tour. A client is counting on it, so you hand it to someone else instead.

1. Find the group tour with your name on it.
2. Select **Substitute**.
3. Pick a member from the list. The list shows only members who can give this tour and are not on it yet.
4. Select **Hand over seat**.

Your seat goes to that member. You, your substitute and your Group's Bookers each get an email.

> **Note:** You can choose a substitute until the group tour starts. After that, ask a Booker or your Scheduler. A Booker or Scheduler can also place or remove anyone.
