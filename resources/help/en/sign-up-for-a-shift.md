# Sign up for a shift

Take a seat on a shift you want to work.

## Sign up

1. Open a Group from the rail.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.
3. Select a schedule to open its shifts.
4. Find a shift with a free seat.
5. Select **Sign up**.

Your name joins the shift, and the seat count goes up by one.

> **Note:** A shift marked **Full** has no free seat. Wait for a seat to open, or pick another shift.

## What next

Read _Cancel a sign-up_ if your plans change. After the shift, read _Record your visitor count after a shift_.
