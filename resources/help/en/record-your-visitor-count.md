# Record your visitor count after a shift

Record how many visitors you served on a shift, and add a note about it if you want.

## Record your shift

1. Open the Group and its **Scheduling** tab.
2. Find your shift in the **My sign-ups** panel at the top.
3. Go to the **Post-shift report** section under the shift details. Your form opens there five minutes before the shift ends.
4. Type the number of visitors in **Visitors served**. Count only the visitors you served, not the ones another Member on the shift served.
5. Type a note in **Comment (optional)** if you have one. Write about visitor questions, how the shift went, or anything you noticed on the floor.
6. Select **Record shift**.

    ![The Post-shift report on your shift in My sign-ups: Visitors served, Comment (optional), and Record shift](01.png)

**Record shift** stays off until you type a number. You can save a count with no comment, but not a comment with no count.

The shift then leaves **My sign-ups**. On the shift in its Schedule, your entry closes to a summary. It shows your count and the first line of your comment. **Last edited by** shows your name and the time.

![Your saved entry in the Schedule: the count, the start of your comment, the Last edited by line, and Change](02.png)

## Change your entry

There is no deadline, so you can fix your entry weeks later.

1. Open the Schedule that holds the shift, and find your entry.
2. Select **Change** on your entry.
3. Fix the count or the comment.
4. Select **Save changes**. To keep what you had, select **Cancel**.

## Read the report

The section lists one entry for each Member on the shift.

- The header counts the entries on file, for example **2 of 3 recorded**.
- **No count yet** means that Member has not recorded the shift.
- You see the other Members' counts. If two of you counted the same visitors, you can spot it and fix it.
- **Last edited by** names the person who saved the entry last. A Scheduler or Chair who corrects your count shows here.

> **Note:** The same section shows on your shift inside its Schedule. Either place saves the same entry.

## Who reads your comment

Only you and your Group's Scheduler and Chair read your comment. Other Members on the shift never see it, and no report shows it.

## Groups that collect more numbers

Some Groups ask for more than one number. The extra boxes show in the same form.

- Tour Groups also show **Visitors served outside the tour**. Fill it in when you served visitors apart from your tour. It is optional.
- GDR asks where the visitors came from. The five boxes are **France and Europe**, **Quebec**, **Toronto**, **Rest of Canada**, and **Other countries**. Fill all five. They must add up to **Visitors served**.
