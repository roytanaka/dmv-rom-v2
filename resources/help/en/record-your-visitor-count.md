# Record your visitor count after a shift

Enter how many visitors you served on a shift you worked.

## Record your count

1. Open the Group and its **Scheduling** tab.
2. Select the schedule that holds the shift.
3. Find your shift. The box opens five minutes before the shift ends.
4. Type the number of visitors in **Visitors served**.
5. Select **Sign out**.

Your count is saved on your seat. There is no deadline, so you can file it later.

> **Tip:** Some Groups show a second box, **Visitors served outside the tour**. Fill it in when you served visitors apart from your tour. It is optional.

## What next

Read _The shifts you still owe a number for_ to catch any shift still missing a count.
