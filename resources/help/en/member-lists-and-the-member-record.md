# Member lists and the Member record

The Records office keeps the department's membership here. You find a Member on a list, then open their record.

## Find a Member

1. Select **Officer Tools**, then **Members**. The **Current** list opens.
2. Select a tab to change the list: **Current**, **Resigned**, **Past**, **Deceased**, **Admin**, **Donors** or **Applicants**.
3. Type the start of a last name in **Search by last name**.
4. Press Enter. The list shows the Members whose last name starts with what you typed.

Each list is sorted by last name. A row shows the name, Category, email, phone and last update.

## Open a Member record

1. Find the Member on a list.
2. Select **Edit**. The Member record opens.
3. Change the fields you need.
4. Select **Save**.

The record shows who last updated it, and when.

> **Note:** You cannot change a Member's Category on the record. Category moves through a dated Category history entry.

## What each list holds

- **Current** holds the department's volunteers, including those on leave.
- **Resigned** holds Resigned and Withdrawn Members.
- **Past** holds Resigned and Deceased Members whose personal data was removed. It stays empty until that happens.
- **Deceased** holds Deceased Members.
- **Admin** holds administrative accounts.
- **Donors** holds Donors.
- **Applicants** opens the Applicants page.
