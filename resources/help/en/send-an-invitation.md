# Send an Invitation

Email your Guests and open the Invitation for Replies.

## Send it

1. Add your Guests first. You cannot send to an empty list.
2. Find the Draft on the **Hosting** tab.
3. Select **Send**.
4. Check the **To** list and select **Next**.
5. Edit the subject and message. The app fills them in from your Invitation.
6. Select **Send**.

The Invitation is now Open. Each Guest gets their own email, with a link to reply. You get a copy.

> **Note:** To stop, close the email panel before you select **Send**. The Invitation stays a Draft.

The email goes out under your name, and replies to it come to you. Your text stays in the language you wrote it in.

## Send it again

Changes send no email on their own. To tell Guests about a change, or to chase a Reply:

1. Find the Invitation on the **Hosting** tab.
2. Select **Re-send**.
3. Select **All Guests** or **Guests with No reply**.
4. Check the **To** list and select **Next**.
5. Edit the subject and message.
6. Select **Send**.

Use **Guests with No reply** to reach a Guest you added after sending.
