# What News is for

News is one feed for the whole DMV. Every signed-in Member reads the same feed.

## Reading news

Select **News** in the top bar. Each item shows a title, the message, the Group that posted it, and the date.

![The News feed: items with a title, the message, the Group that posted it, and the date](01.png)

## Posting news

Most Members read the feed but do not post to it. A News Editor posts on behalf of a Group. The article "Post a news item" covers posting.
