# Return to yourself after impersonating

Leave a Persona and get back to your own account.

## Return to yourself

1. Find the toolbar in the bottom-right corner. It shows the Persona you became.

    ![The toolbar while you act as a Persona: their name and roles, Switch, and Stop](01.png)

2. Select **Stop**.

The app reloads as you. Your own roles and standing come back.

## Switch to another Persona

To become someone else without stopping first, select **Switch** in the toolbar. Then select the next Persona.

![Switch open: the list of Personas above the toolbar](02.png)

> **Note:** **Stop** always returns you to the account you started from, whatever the Persona's roles.
