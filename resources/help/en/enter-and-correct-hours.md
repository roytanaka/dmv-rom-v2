# Enter and correct hours

Add the hours you worked for a Group outside your Shifts, or fix a number already on file. Any Member can do this for a Group whose page they can open.

## How hours reach a record

You do not enter every kind of hour:

- **Scheduled hours** come from your Shifts. The app counts them for you.
- **Meeting hours** come from meetings you attended.
- **Extra hours** are the only kind you type in. They cover work outside Shifts and meetings.

## Enter extra hours

1. Open the Group from the rail.
2. Select the **Hours** tab.
3. Find the month you want under **Record extra hours**.
4. Type the hours in **Hours to add**.
5. Select **Add hours**.

The form offers this month and last month. On file shows what is already recorded.

> **Note:** Do not add Shifts or meetings. The app already counts them.

## Correct a number

Type a negative number in **Hours to add** to lower the total. The number is added to what is on file, so a negative one subtracts. The total never drops below zero.

## What next

Your months show in "Your hours in this group" below the form. A Chair or Statistician reads the whole Group in the hours report.
