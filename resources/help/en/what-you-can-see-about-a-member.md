# What you can and cannot see about a Member

A Member's profile shows different details to different people. This article explains what you see and why.

## What everyone sees

Any signed-in Member sees these details on a profile:

- Name
- Photo
- Standing
- Groups and roles

## Contact details

Email and phone numbers are not for everyone. You see them only when you are an officer of a Group the Member belongs to. If you may not see them, the profile reads "Contact details are not available to you."

## What stays private

Some details never show to a peer:

- The home address
- Skills

Only the Member themselves and DMV Records see these. Your own home address and skills sit in your Settings.
