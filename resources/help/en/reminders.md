# Reminders: what you get and when

The app emails you about shifts you signed up for. You set nothing up.

## Shift reminders

A shift reminder arrives a few days before a shift you hold a seat on. It names the shift and its time. Your Group sets the lead time, and three days is the usual.

## When you get one

You get one reminder for each shift you signed up for. The email comes in the language you chose. A shift you already dropped sends you none.

> **Note:** A Group can turn shift reminders off. If yours does, you get no reminders from that Group.
