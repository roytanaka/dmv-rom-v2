# Pick your Guests

Build the guest list for your Invitation, one Member at a time or by whole Group.

## Add Guests

1. Find the Invitation on the **Hosting** tab.
2. Select **Guests**.

    ![The Guests page with Add a Member, Add a group, and no Guests yet](01.png)

3. To add one Member, pick a name under **Add a Member** and select **Add**.
4. To add a whole Group, pick it under **Add a group** and select **Add**.

The list shows every Guest and the total.

![The Guests page with 24 Guests added from Docents](02.png)

**Add a group** lists the Groups you belong to. An org-wide sender also sees every Group and All members.

Only current Members can be Guests: Active, Honourary, Sustaining, and LOA. Provisional and PreActive Members cannot be Guests. A Group or All members also leaves out Members on leave. Add them one at a time.

## Remove a Guest

1. Select **Remove** beside the name.
2. Confirm.

Their Reply goes with them.

> **Note:** A Guest you add after sending sees the Invitation on My invitations, but gets no email. A Guest with the no-email flag gets no mail at all.

## What next

Read [Send an Invitation](send-an-invitation) to open it to your Guests.
