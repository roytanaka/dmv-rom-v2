# Correct Earned on a group tour

Replace a group tour's Earned when the figure the app works out is wrong. You need the Statistician or Chair role.

The app works out Earned from the booking type's rates. It adds the rate per visitor times the visitors to the rate per docent-hour times the docent-hours. Docent-hours are the docents signed up times the length of the tour.

## Correct Earned

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Select the **Scheduling** tab.
3. Open the month's group-tour schedule.
4. Find the group tour. Its Earned line says **worked out** or **corrected**.
5. Select the pencil beside Earned.
6. Enter the correct amount.
7. Select **Save**.

The group tour now shows your amount, marked **corrected**. Later changes to the group tour keep your amount.

## Go back to the worked-out figure

1. Select the pencil beside Earned.
2. Select **Clear correction**.

The group tour shows the worked-out figure again.

> **Note:** A correction of 0 is kept. Clear the correction to go back to the worked-out figure.
