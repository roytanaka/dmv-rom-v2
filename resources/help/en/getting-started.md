# Your first login

This app is where DMV Volunteers work online. You track your hours, sign up for Shifts, and keep up with your Groups.

This page shows you around. Each other article covers one task.

![The dashboard: the black top bar across the top, the Left Side Bar with your Groups, and your Groups in the middle](01.png)

## The top bar

The black bar stays at the top of every page. It holds your main links: My Hours, My Calendar, News, and Directory. Your initials sit on the right. Help, marked with a "?", sits beside them.

## The Left Side Bar

The Left Side Bar lists the Groups you belong to. Select a Group to open its pages.

## Your Groups

Your Groups sit in the Left Side Bar under My Groups. Each Group has its own News, Schedule, and roster. Open a Group to see a Shift you can take.

## Where to find help

Select Help in the top bar. Help opens the article for the page you are on. Each article gives you short steps to follow.
