# Getting started

This app is where DMV Volunteers work online. You track your hours, sign up for Shifts, and keep up with your Groups.

This page shows you around. Each other article covers one task.

## The top bar

The black bar stays at the top of every page. It holds your main links: My Hours, My Calendar, News, and Directory. Your name sits on the right. The "?" beside it opens Help.

## The rail

The rail runs down the left side. It lists the Groups you belong to. Select a Group to open its pages.

## Your Groups

Your Groups sit in the rail under My Groups. Each Group has its own News, Schedule, and roster. Open a Group to see a Shift you can take.

## Where to find help

Select the "?" in the top bar. Help opens the article for the page you are on. Each article gives you short steps to follow.
