# Email Accepted Guests

Write to everyone who accepted an Invitation. The page shows no email addresses.

1. Find the Invitation on the **Hosting** tab.
2. Select **Replies**.
3. Select **Email Accepted Guests**.
4. Check the **To** list and select **Next**.
5. Write your subject and message.
6. Select **Send**.

## What next

The app sends each Accepted Guest a Reminder by email 4 days before the occasion. Read [Send an Invitation](send-an-invitation) to mail a change to all your Guests.
