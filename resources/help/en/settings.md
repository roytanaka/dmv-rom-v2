# What Settings are for

Settings is where you manage your own account. Select your initials or photo in the top bar, then **My Profile**.

![Settings: the sidebar with Profile, Password, and Skills, and the Profile page open](01.png)

## Profile

The Profile page holds your name, email, contact details, and photo. This is what other Members and your Group officers see.

## Password

The Password page changes your sign-in password.

## Skills

The Skills page records the skills you are willing to use for the DMV. Your choices stay private, seen only by you and DMV Records.

## Moving between them

The sidebar lists Profile, Password, and Skills. Select one to open it. Read [Update your profile and photo](update-your-profile), [Change your password](change-your-password), and [Record your skills](record-your-skills) for the steps on each page.
