# Pick an Audience

Choose who a Broadcast goes to. The app builds the list of Members for you.

## Open the menu

1. Select **Email** on a Group page, a roster, a Schedule, or a Shift.
2. Read the menu. Each line names an Audience and the number of Members it reaches.
3. Select the Audience you want.

## What each Audience means

- **Whole group** reaches every Member in present standing. Any member of the Group may pick it.
- **The group's officers** reaches everyone who holds a role in the Group.
- **Sign-ups on this shift** reaches the Members signed up for that Shift.
- **All members** and the other org-wide sets reach the whole DMV. Only Records may pick these.

## Change the list

The **To** field shows one chip per Member. Remove anyone with the × beside their name. The Audience name stays, with a note of how many you removed.

> **Tip:** Select **Pick people…** to build a list by hand from the page's roster.
