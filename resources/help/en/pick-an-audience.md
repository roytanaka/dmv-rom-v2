# Pick an Audience

Choose who a Broadcast goes to. The app builds the list of Members for you.

## Open the menu

1. Select **Email** on a Group page, a Schedule, a Shift, or the Directory.

    ![The Email menu on a Group page: the roster Audiences, the officers, the child Groups, and Pick people](01.png)

2. Read the menu. Each line names an Audience and the number of Members it reaches.
3. Select the Audience you want.

## What each Audience means

- **Whole group** reaches every Member in present standing. **Whole group and on leave** adds the Members on leave. Any member of the Group may pick them.
- A standing, such as **Full** or **Trainee**, reaches the Members of the Group with that standing. **Active** reaches the Members with Full standing.
- **The group's officers** reaches everyone who holds a role in the Group. A child Group's name reaches that Group's roster. Only the Group's officers may pick these.
- **Sign-ups on this shift** reaches the Members signed up for that Shift. Only the Scheduler or the Chair may pick it.
- **All members** and the other org-wide Audiences reach the whole DMV. They are on the Directory's **Email** menu. Only a Chair, a member of the Executive, Records, or Awards group, or a super-tier officer may pick them.
- **Board of Directors**, **Committee Chairs**, and **All Chairs** are on the Directory's **Email** menu too. Any Member may pick them.

> **Note:** On the DMV group itself, every Audience reaches the whole DMV. The org-wide rule decides who may pick them there.

## Change the list

The **To** field shows one name for each Member. Remove anyone with the × beside their name. The Audience name stays, with a note of how many you removed.

![The Who step after one removal: Whole group, 1 removed](02.png)

## Pick people by hand

The **Email** menu is on every tab of a Group page. Open **Pick people…** from any tab.

1. Select **Email**.
2. Select **Pick people…**.

    ![Pick people: an empty To field above the roster, each Member with a box to tick](03.png)

3. Tick the Members you want. To find a Member fast, search by name.
