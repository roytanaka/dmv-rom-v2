# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home page. It opens when you sign in.

## My Groups

The Dashboard shows a tile for each Group you belong to. Each tile carries the Group's name and mark. Select a tile to open that Group.

If you belong to no Group, this area stays empty.

## The rest of the app

The top bar holds My Hours, My Calendar, News, and Directory. The rail on the left lists your Groups too. Your name sits on the right, with the "?" for Help beside it.
