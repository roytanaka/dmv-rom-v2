# What Groups are for

A Group is how DMV organizes its people. Every committee, program, and project is a Group. You belong to some, and you can look at the rest.

## My Groups

The Left Side Bar lists the Groups you belong to under **My Groups**. Select a Group to open its page.

![The Left Side Bar: My Groups at the top, Browse Groups below it](01.png)

## Browse Groups

**Browse Groups** sits below My Groups in the Left Side Bar. It holds the other Groups you can look at, sorted into a few areas. Open one to read about a Group you have not joined.

## A Group's page

A Group page gathers what that Group does. Tabs across the top hold its roster, meetings, schedule, and hours. Every Member sees the same tabs.

![A Group page: the banner, then the tabs across the top](02.png)

Each other article in this section covers one task. Start with _Find your Group's page_.
