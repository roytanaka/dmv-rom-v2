# Find your Group's settings

Open the **Settings** tab to change how your Group runs. You need the Scheduler or Chair role.

## Open the Settings tab

1. Open your Group from the rail.
2. Select the **Settings** tab. It is the last tab in the strip.

The tab shows one card for each setting you can change.

## What the tab holds

A setting is a value you set once. The app uses it from then on. The tab holds five cards:

- **Shift reminders** turns reminder emails on and sets how many days' notice they give.
- **Empty-desk alert** emails your Group about watched Shifts with no one signed up.
- **Self-serve shifts** lets Members write their own Shifts.
- **Shift kinds** holds the list of kinds your Group offers.
- **Objects** holds the collection Members take onto the floor.

The five cards show only when your Group runs scheduling.

## Where the settings used to be

These cards used to sit on the **Scheduling** tab. They moved here so the Scheduling tab opens on your Schedules.

Schedules, Shifts and sign-ups stay on the **Scheduling** tab. You still create and edit them there.

> **Note:** Only a Member who can change a setting sees the Settings tab. Other Members do not see it.

## What next

Read _Set Reminders and the empty-desk alert for your Group_ to fill in the first two cards.
