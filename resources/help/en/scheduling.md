# What Scheduling is for

Scheduling is how a Group staffs its shifts. You sign up for the shifts you want to work.

## Schedules

A Schedule is a named date range a Group publishes its shifts in, often one month. Open the **Scheduling** tab to see a Group's schedules.

![The Scheduling tab: the list of schedules below your sign-ups, one card each with its date range](01.png)

## Shifts

A Shift is a dated slot you sign up to staff, such as a tour, a desk slot, or an event role. Each Shift has a start time, an end time, and a capacity.

## Capacity

Capacity is how many Members a Shift holds. A Shift shows its seats taken against its capacity, so you can tell when one is full.

![A schedule's agenda: each shift shows its seats taken against its capacity, and a full shift says Full](02.png)

## Sign-ups

A Sign-up is your place on a Shift. You take a seat by signing up, and you give it back by dropping it. The **My sign-ups** panel at the top of the Scheduling tab lists your upcoming shifts.
