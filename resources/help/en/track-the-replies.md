# Track the Replies

See who is coming and print the list for the door.

1. Find the Invitation on the **Hosting** tab.
2. Select **Replies**. The top of the page shows the totals for Accepted, Declined, No reply, and each Option.
3. Read the list below the totals. It shows each Guest with their Reply and Options. Accepted Guests come first.
4. Select **Print** to print the totals and the list.

You get one email when the Accepted Replies reach your Guest limit.
