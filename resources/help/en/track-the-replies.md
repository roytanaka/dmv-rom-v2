# Track the Replies

See who is coming, record answers you get in person, and print the list for the door.

## Read the Replies

1. Find the Invitation on the **Hosting** tab.
2. Select **Replies**.

The top of the page shows the totals for Accepted, Declined, No reply, and each Option. The list below shows each Guest with their Reply and Options. Accepted Guests come first.

You get one email when the Accepted Replies reach your Guest limit.

## Set a Reply for a Guest

Use this for an answer given by phone or at the door. It works after the Invitation is Closed.

1. Select **Set reply** beside the Guest.
2. Pick **Accepted** or **Declined**.
3. If you pick **Accepted**, tick the Guest's Options.
4. Select **Save**.

To answer for a Member who is not on the list, select **Reply for a Member** and pick their name. They become a Guest.

> **Note:** You can go over your own Guest limit. The limit stops only Guests who reply for themselves.

## Print or email

- Select **Print** to print the totals and the list.
- Select **Email Accepted Guests** to write to everyone who accepted. The page shows no email addresses.

## What next

The app sends each Accepted Guest a Reminder by email 4 days before the occasion. Read [Send an Invitation](send-an-invitation) to mail a change to your Guests.
