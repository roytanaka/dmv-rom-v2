# Edit your Group's About Us and banner

Change the description and the join steps on your Group's page, and pick its banner image. You need the Secretary or Chair role.

## Edit the About Us text

1. Open your Group from the Left Side Bar.
2. Stay on the **Overview** tab.
3. Select **Edit** in the **About Us** card.

    ![The Overview tab: Edit on the About Us card, and Change banner on the banner](01.png)

4. Type your description in the box.
5. Select **Save**.

    ![The About Us edit form: the text box, Save, and Cancel](02.png)

> **Note:** Empty the box and save to clear the description.

## Write the How to join steps

The **How to join** card tells other Members how to join your Group. Every Member who opens your Group can read it.

1. On the **Overview** tab, find the **How to join** card below **At a glance**.
2. Select **Edit** in the **How to join** card.
3. Type the steps to join, one per line.
4. Select **Save**.

> **Note:** Empty the box and save to clear the steps. The card then tells readers to contact your Group's Chair.

## Choose a banner

1. Select **Change banner** at the top right of the banner.
2. The picker opens with six images.

    ![The Choose a banner dialog with its six banners](03.png)

3. Select the banner you want.

Your pick saves right away. The picker closes and your Group shows the new banner.

## What next

Your Group's Members see the new About Us text and banner on the Overview tab. Every Member sees the How to join card.
