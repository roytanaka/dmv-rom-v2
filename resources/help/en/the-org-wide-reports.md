# The org-wide reports

Read the hours reports that cover every Group in DMV. You need the super-tier grant or the Records role.

## Open the reports

Go to a DMV hours report from its address, then use the links across the top to move between them. Every report covers one fiscal year. Set the year under **Fiscal year**.

## What each report shows

- **Summary** shows scheduled hours by committee, month by month. Meeting hours, extra hours, and a grand total sit below.
- **Detailed** splits each committee into four rows: shifts, meetings, extra hours, and visitor interactions.
- **Visitor interactions** shows the visitor count each Group recorded. Any Member can read this one.
- **Ranked hours** lists active and provisional Members by total hours, most first. A second list names those with no hours yet.
- **Zero hours** lists Members with no hours at all this year.
- **Zero shift hours** lists Members with no scheduled hours.
- **Zero extra hours** lists Members with no extra hours.

## Export or print

Select **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet, or **Print** for a paper copy. Read _Export a report as CSV_ for more.

> **Note:** A figure marked **Incomplete** reads low. Group bookings are not counted yet.
