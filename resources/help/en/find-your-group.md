# Find your Group's page

Open the page for a Group you belong to, or one you want to read about.

## Open a Group you belong to

1. Look at the rail on the left.
2. Find the Group under **My Groups**.
3. Select its name. The Group page opens.

## Find another Group

1. Select **Browse Groups** in the rail.
2. The list opens into a few areas.
3. Select an area to see its Groups.
4. Select a Group to open its page.

> **Tip:** Type a name in the **Filter groups** box at the top of the rail. The rail narrows to Groups that match.

## What next

On a Group page, read _The tabs on a Group page_ to learn what each tab holds.
