# Find your Group's page

Open the page for a Group you belong to, or one you want to read about.

## Open a Group you belong to

1. Look at the Left Side Bar.
2. Find the Group under **My Groups**.

    ![The Left Side Bar: the Groups you belong to, listed under My Groups](01.png)

3. Select its name. The Group page opens.

    ![The Group page that opens: the banner and the tabs across the top](02.png)

## Find another Group

1. Select **Browse Groups** in the Left Side Bar.
2. The list opens into a few areas.

    ![Browse Groups expanded in the Left Side Bar: the areas the other Groups sort into](03.png)

3. Select an area to see its Groups.
4. Select a Group to open its page.

> **Tip:** Type a name in the **Filter groups** box at the top of the Left Side Bar. The list narrows to Groups that match.

## What next

On a Group page, read [The tabs on a Group page](group-page-tabs) to learn what each tab holds.
