# Read news

Read the news that Groups across the DMV have posted.

## Open the feed

1. Select **News** in the top bar.
2. The feed opens with the newest item first.

## Read an item

Each item shows:

- The title
- The message
- The Group that posted it, and the date

If no Group has posted yet, the feed reads "No news yet."

## What next

Do you post news for a Group? Read "Post a news item".
